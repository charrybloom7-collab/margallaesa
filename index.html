Where Is Margalla Enclave Islamabad Located?

Margalla Enclave Islamabad is located along Jinnah Avenue, formerly known as Kuri Road, in Zone IV of the capital. The project site is situated near Mouza Kuri and Rehara and is accessible through several connecting roads, including Park Road, Kuri Road, Malot Road, and Jinnah Avenue.

Nearby Areas and Surroundings

Margalla Enclave is surrounded by several existing landmarks and residential developments. According to the project’s Environmental Impact Assessment, Park View City lies to the northeast, the Botanical Garden is to the north, Bahria Enclave is to the south, and Rehara Village is to the west.
